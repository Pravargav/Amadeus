## 1. Question: What does "It might have happened" mean?

**Options:**

a) The possibility that it happened in the past is 50%.

b) It probably happened.

c) Something happened, but now we hope something else happened.

## Answer:

The correct answer is closest to **(b)**, but it is more accurate to say:

**"It is possible that it happened in the past."**

Examples:
- He might have missed the bus.
- She might have forgotten my birthday.
- It might have rained last night.

**Explanation of the options:**

| Option | Correct? | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | No | Might does not indicate an exact 50% probability. |
| (b) | Partially | It indicates a possibility, but not necessarily a strong probability. |
| (c) | No | It does not express hope for a different outcome. |

---

## 2. Question: Give sentences for meanings (a) and (c).

## Answer (a): Possibility is approximately 50%

There is no exact grammatical structure for 50% probability. We can express uncertainty using *might* or *could*.

Examples:
1. He might come to the party.
2. It might rain tomorrow.
3. She could be at home.
4. They might accept our proposal.

**Meaning:** These sentences express possibility, not an exact 50% probability.

## Answer (c): Something happened, but now we hope something else happened.

We can use expressions such as *I hope* and *I wish*.

Examples:

1. **I hope he hasn't missed the train.**
   - I don't know whether he missed it.
   - I hope he caught it.

2. **I wish he had attended the meeting.**
   - He didn't attend the meeting.
   - I regret that.

3. **I hope she has reached home safely.**
   - She travelled home.
   - I hope she arrived safely.

4. **I hope it didn't rain yesterday.**
   - It may have rained.
   - I hope it didn't.

### Important difference

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| It might have happened. | Maybe it happened. |
| I hope it happened. | I want it to have happened. |
| I wish it had happened. | I regret that it didn't happen. |
| I hope it hasn't happened. | I want to believe it didn't happen. |

**Remember:** *Might have + V3* expresses a possibility about the past, whereas *hope* and *wish* express our desires or feelings about an outcome.
