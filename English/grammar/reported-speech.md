
## Reported Speech: ##

---

### 🔁 Common Rules (Apply to all types)

###  Change of Pronouns

| Direct | Reported |
|------|---------|
| I | he / she |
| we | they |
| my | his / her |
| you | I / he / she |


###  Change of Time & Place Words

| Direct | Reported |
|------|---------|
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day |
| here | there |

---

### 1️⃣ Assertive (Declarative) Sentences

### 🔹 Reporting Verbs
- **said** (no object)
- **told** (object required)

### 🔹 Rule
- Remove quotation marks 
- Add **that** (optional) 
- Change tense, pronoun, and time/place words 
---

### 2️⃣ Imperative Sentences (Order / Request / Advice / Warning)


### 🔹 Reporting Verbs

| Type | Verb Used |
|----|----------|
| Order | ordered, commanded |
| Request | requested, asked |
| Advice | advised |
| Warning | warned |

### 🔹 Rule
- Remove quotation marks 
- Use **to + verb** 
- Negative → **not to + verb**

---

### 3️⃣ Interrogative Sentences (Questions)


### 🔹 Reporting Verbs
- asked
- inquired
- wanted to know

### 🔹 Types of Questions

###  A. Yes / No Questions 
Use **if / whether**

- **Direct:** He said, “Are you ready?” 
  **Indirect:** He asked **if** I was ready.

###  B. Wh-Questions 
Use the same **wh-word** (what, when, where, why, how)

- **Direct:** She said, “Where do you live?” 
  **Indirect:** She asked where I lived.

### 🔹 Important Rules
- No question mark 
- No helping verb (do/does/did/is/are) 
- Use **statement word order**
- You should not use **that** 
---

### 4️⃣ Exclamatory Sentences

### 🔹 Reporting Verbs
- exclaimed
- exclaimed with joy / sorrow
- wished

### 🔹 Rule
- Remove interjections (Oh!, Alas!, Hurrah!) 
- Convert emotion into words 

---


***Note:-***  *All indirect speech in past tense so while using indirect speech use past tense.*
