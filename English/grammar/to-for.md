The correct form is:
 
✅ **used to wash**  
❌ **used for wash**
 
Because after **“used for”**, we normally use a **noun** or **verb + ing** form.
 
## Correct patterns:
 
- **used to + verb**
- **used for + verb-ing / noun**
 
## Examples:
 
- This soap is **used to wash** clothes. ✅
- This soap is **used for washing** clothes. ✅
 
❌ “used for wash” is grammatically incorrect because **wash** is a base verb here.
