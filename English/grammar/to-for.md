

## Difference Between “To” and “For”

## 🔹 To
Shows **direction, transfer, or action**.

Usually followed by:
- a **base verb**
- a **person/place**

### Pattern
- to + verb
- to + person/place

### Examples
- I want **to eat**.
- This soap is used **to wash** clothes.
- Give it **to me**.


## 🔹 For
Shows **purpose, benefit, reason, or duration**.

Usually followed by:
- a **noun**
- a **verb + ing**

### Pattern
- for + noun
- for + verb-ing

### Examples
- This soap is **for clothes**.
- This soap is used **for washing** clothes.
- I bought this **for you**
