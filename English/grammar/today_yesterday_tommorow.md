Emphasise the day (today / yesterday / tomorrow) in British English ✅

---

🇬🇧 1️⃣ Emphasising TODAY

I completed it earlier today.

I’ve completed it today. ⭐ (very British)

---

🇬🇧 2️⃣ Emphasising YESTERDAY

I completed it just yesterday.

I did complete it yesterday. ⭐ (strong emphasis)

I completed it as early as yesterday.

---

🇬🇧 3️⃣ Emphasising TOMORROW

I’ll definitely finish it tomorrow.

I am completing it tomorrow. ⭐ (confident British tone)
