trade for vs trade with

They have different meanings.

1. trade A for B

= give up A and get B in exchange

Your sentence:

He is trading his job for a life of enjoyment. ✅

Meaning:

He is giving up his job.
In return, he gets a life of enjoyment.

More examples:

She traded her car for a motorcycle.
He traded city life for country life.
They traded stability for freedom.

Pattern:

trade something for something else

2. trade with someone

= do business or exchange goods/services with a person, company, or country

Examples:

India trades with Singapore.
The company trades with several European partners.
We have been trading with them for years.

Pattern:

trade with a person/company/country

Compare

He traded his job for a life of enjoyment. ✅

He gave up one thing and got another.

He trades with his employer. ✅

He does business or exchanges something with his employer.

Notice that for focuses on what is exchanged, while with focuses on who the exchange is with.

A useful memory trick:

for = in exchange for what?
with = with whom? ✅
