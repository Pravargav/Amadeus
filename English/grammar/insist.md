Use insisted on when talking about an

action or thing someone strongly 

demands.

Structure:

👉 insist on + noun / verb-ing

Examples:

She insisted on paying the bill.

He insisted on coming with us.

They insisted on immediate action.

My friend insisted on driving.

👉 Focus = the action

---

Use insisted that when giving a 

statement, opinion, or demand.

Structure:

👉 insist that + subject + verb

Examples:

She insisted that she was right.

He insisted that the report was 

correct.

The doctor insisted that he take rest.
They insisted that we stay longer.
👉 Focus = a full idea or statement
---
Used when the object or idea is already understood from context.
Examples:
“Are you sure?” — “Yes,” he insisted.
She refused to listen, but he insisted.
I said no, but they insisted.
👉 Meaning is complete from previous sentence.
