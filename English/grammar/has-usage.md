
# Uses of "Has" in English

**"Has" is used for humans, animals, and objects too.** It is not limited to humans.

We use **has** with **he, she, it, and singular nouns** in the present tense.

## 1. With Humans

- He **has** a car.
- She **has** two brothers.
- Rahul **has** a new job.

## 2. With Animals

- The dog **has** a long tail.
- The cat **has** blue eyes.

## 3. With Objects

- My laptop **has** a touchscreen.
- The house **has** three bedrooms.
- This phone **has** a good camera.

## 4. With Situations and Events

- The meeting **has** started.
- The weather **has** changed.
- My laptop **has** stopped working.

---

## Quick Rule

Use **has** with:
- **He**
- **She**
- **It**
- **Singular nouns**

Use **have** with:
- **I**
- **You**
- **We**
- **They**
- **Plural nouns**
