
## 1\. Faster / Quicker

| Word or Phrase | Example |
| --- | --- |
| More rapidly | He completes tasks more rapidly than I do. |
| More quickly | She learns more quickly than her colleagues. |
| At a higher speed | This machine operates at a higher speed. |
| In less time | I can finish this in less time than before. |
| More efficiently | He processes data more efficiently than me. |
| Swiftly | The new system responds more swiftly. |
| At a faster pace | She works at a faster pace than I do. |
| Earlier | I finished the task earlier than expected. |
| Sooner | We reached the destination sooner than expected. |
| With greater speed | This application loads with greater speed. |

## 2\. Slower

| Word or Phrase | Example |
| --- | --- |
| More slowly | He writes more slowly than me. |
| At a lower speed | This vehicle runs at a lower speed. |
| At a slower pace | I'm progressing at a slower pace than my peers. |
| Less rapidly | The economy is growing less rapidly. |
| Taking longer | This process is taking longer than expected. |
| Less efficiently | The old system operates less efficiently. |
| Gradually | The system is responding more gradually. |
| Behind schedule | We are progressing behind schedule. |
| Lagging behind | Our team is lagging behind other teams. |

## 3\. Natural Comparative Sentence Patterns

Useful patterns for professional and everyday conversations.

| Comparison | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Faster than | He learns faster than I do. |
| Quicker than | She finishes assignments quicker than me. |
| Slower than | My laptop runs slower than yours. |
| More efficiently than | He solves problems more efficiently than I do. |
| Less efficiently than | The old process works less efficiently than the new one. |
| Takes less time than | This approach takes less time than the previous one. |
| Takes longer than | This task takes longer than expected. |
| At a much faster pace | Technology is evolving at a much faster pace. |

## 4\. Advanced Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accelerate | Become faster | We need to accelerate the development process. |
| Decelerate | Become slower | The vehicle began to decelerate. |
| Expedite | Make a process happen sooner | Could you expedite the approval process? |
| Hasten | Make something happen faster | We should hasten the implementation. |
| Outpace | Progress faster than someone or something | Our competitors are outpacing us. |
| Lag behind | Progress more slowly than others | Our team is lagging behind. |
| Streamline | Make a process simpler and more efficient | We need to streamline the workflow. |
| Prolong | Make something last longer | Technical issues prolonged the deployment. |

## 5\. Important Differences

| Expression | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Faster | Higher speed |
| Quicker | Takes less time |
| More efficiently | Achieves better results with less time or effort |
| Sooner | Happens earlier |
| Slower | Lower speed or takes more time |
