# Despite vs However

**Despite** and **however** are both used to show contrast, but they are used differently in a sentence.

---

## 1. Despite

“Despite” is a **preposition**. It is followed by a **noun**, **pronoun**, or **verb+ing**.

### Structure

```text
Despite + noun / verb-ing
```

### Examples

- Despite the rain, we played cricket.
- Despite being tired, he continued working.
- Despite her efforts, she failed.

### Meaning

**Even though**

---

## 2. However

“However” is a **conjunctive adverb** used to connect two contrasting ideas.

### Structure

```text
Sentence. However, sentence.
```

### Examples

- It was raining. However, we played cricket.
- He was tired. However, he continued working.
- She worked hard. However, she failed.

### Meaning

**But / Nevertheless**

---

## Quick Comparison

| Despite | However |
|---|---|
| Followed by noun or verb-ing | Connects two full sentences |
| Preposition | Conjunctive adverb |
| “Despite the rain…” | “However, it was raining…” |
| No subject + verb immediately after | Usually followed by a comma |

---

## Example Comparison

- Despite the traffic, I reached on time.
- There was heavy traffic. However, I reached on time.
