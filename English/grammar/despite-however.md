# Despite vs However

Both show **contrast**, but they work differently in a sentence.

## 1. Despite = “even though / in spite of”

### Despite + noun

- Despite **the rain**, we went out.
- Despite **his illness**, he came to work.
- Despite **the difficulties**, she finished the project.

### Despite + -ing

- Despite **being tired**, I continued working.
- Despite **having little experience**, he got the job.

❌ Despite it was raining, we went out.  
✅ Despite **the fact that it was raining**, we went out.  
✅ **Although** it was raining, we went out.

---

## 2. However = “but / nevertheless”

However usually connects two contrasting statements.

- It was raining. **However,** we went out.
- He is inexperienced. **However,** he learns quickly.
- The task was difficult. **However,** I managed to finish it.
