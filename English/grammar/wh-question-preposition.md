-> Dialy usage Structure

WH-word + auxiliary verb + subject + main 
verb + preposition

👉 WH + aux + subject + verb + preposition ?

 Examples:

What are you looking for?

Whom are you reporting to?

Which company are you working for?

Where did you come from?

---

-> Formal Structure (Preposition before WH-word)

In formal English, the preposition comes 
before the WH-word.

Structure:

👉 Preposition + WH-word + auxiliary + subject + verb

Examples:

To whom are you reporting? (formal)

For what are you looking? (very formal)

With whom did you speak?
