-> ***Process (Process Variant / Step)*** - 
A Process is a single step within a Process Chain. It is the modularization of a technical action (e.g., "Delete Data from Target", "Activate Data"). 

-> ***Process Chain (RSPC)*** - 
A Process Chain is a sequence of Processes (steps) that are scheduled to wait for an event, automatically triggering the next step upon successful completion of the previous one. 

|---|

-> Process Chain is the container (functional).

-> Background Job is the executor (technical).

|---|

-> Processes: Monitored via RSPC, providing functional insights, logs, and dependencies.

-> Jobs: Monitored via SM37, showing technical status, duration, and user.

|---|

-> SAP BW Processes (specifically Process Chains) are visual, automated sequences used to model and schedule data loading/transformation (ETL) workflows in SAP. BW Jobs (often via transaction SM37) are the underlying background background tasks generated by the system to execute these steps. Processes are user-defined workflows, while Jobs are technical execution units.
