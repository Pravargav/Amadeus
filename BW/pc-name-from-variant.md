# Steps to Retrieve and Display the Variant

1. Open transaction **SE38**.
2. In the Program field, enter:


RSPROCESS

(instead of `/SSA/BWT`).

3. Instead of clicking **Execute**, click the **Variants** button located in the top-center toolbar.

4. Open a **new SAP window** and navigate to **SE38**.

5. In the Program field, enter:


/SSA/BWT

and execute it.

6. Navigate to the required **Process Chain**.

7. Click on the **Process Chain ID**.

8. Locate and copy the **displayed Variant Name**.

9. Return to the first SE38 window (where `RSPROCESS` is open).

10. Paste the copied Variant Name into the **Variant** field.

11. Click the **Display** button (Spectacles icon) to view the variant details.
