### SAP Password Reset Steps

1. Contact an associate and request a temporary password.

2. Open **SAP Logon**, right-click on the **BD1** system, and select **Without SSO**.

3. On the login screen that appears, enter your **User ID** and the **temporary password**, then press **Enter**.

4. You will be prompted to reset your password.

5. Enter and confirm your new password, then proceed with the login.
