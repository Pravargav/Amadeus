# 📊 How Often Companies Do Budget, Planning, Forecast & Rolling Forecast

---

## 1️⃣ Budget

### ✅ What it is
- A **formal financial target**
- Approved by **management**
- Baseline for **performance measurement**

### ⏱️ Frequency (Majority of companies)
- **Yearly**
- Prepared **once a year** for the next fiscal year

### 📆 Typical timeline
- Prepared: **Q3–Q4 of current year**
- Valid for: **Next full financial year**

### 🧪 Example
- In **Oct–Nov 2025**
- Company prepares **Budget 2026**
- Budget period: **Jan 2026 – Dec 2026**

📌 *Rare case:* Some startups revise budgets mid-year, but **yearly is the default**.

---

## 2️⃣ Planning (Operational / Financial Planning)

### ✅ What it is
- Broader than budget
- Includes:
  - Headcount
  - Capex
  - Sales plans
  - Cost plans
- Less rigid than budget

### ⏱️ Frequency (Majority)
- **Yearly**
- Reviewed **quarterly** if needed

### 📆 Typical timeline
- Annual planning cycle
- Adjusted during the year based on **strategy changes**

### 🧪 Example
- Annual **Headcount Plan**
- Annual **Sales Plan by Region**
- Annual **IT Spend Plan**

📌 *Think of Planning as* **how to achieve the budget**.

---

## 3️⃣ Forecast

### ✅ What it is
- **Updated prediction** of the future
- Based on **actuals + trends**
- Compared against **budget**

### ⏱️ Frequency (Majority)
- **Monthly**
- Sometimes **Quarterly** (less mature companies)

### 📆 Typical timeline
- After **month-end close**
- Uses **latest actual data**

### 🧪 Example
- Budget 2026 revenue = **₹100 Cr**
- After Jan–Mar actuals:
  - New forecast = **₹95 Cr**

📌 *Forecast answers:*  
> “Given what we know today, where will we land?”

---

## 4️⃣ Rolling Forecast

### ✅ What it is
- A **continuous forecast**
- Always looks ahead a fixed period:
  - 12 / 15 / 18 months
- Replaces static forecasting

### ⏱️ Frequency (Majority using it)
- **Monthly update**
- Forecast window **moves forward every month**

### 📆 Typical timeline
- Jan update → Forecast **Feb–Jan next year**
- Feb update → Forecast **Mar–Feb next year**

### 🧪 Example (12-Month Rolling Forecast)

| Month     | Forecast Period        |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Jan 2026  | Feb 2026 – Jan 2027    |
| Feb 2026  | Mar 2026 – Feb 2027    |

📌 *Common in:*
- Large enterprises
- Fast-changing industries
- FP&A-driven organizations
