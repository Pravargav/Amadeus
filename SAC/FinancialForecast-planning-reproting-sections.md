# Reporting Section – Financial Forecast (SAP Analytics Cloud)

The **Reporting** section is used for **analysis, performance tracking, and decision-making**. 
It consumes **published planning data** and presents it in structured dashboards.



## 1️⃣ Overview

### Purpose
Provides a **high-level snapshot** of financial performance.

### What It Shows
- Key financial KPIs
- Revenue, costs, margin, EBITDA, cash flow (high level)
- Aggregated view for quick decision-making


---

## 2️⃣ Version Comparison

### Purpose
Compares **multiple planning versions** side by side.

### Typical Comparisons
- Budget vs Forecast
- Forecast V1 vs Forecast V2
- Best Case vs Worst Case


---

## 3️⃣ vs Actuals PY & BUD

### Meaning
- **PY** → Previous Year Actuals 
- **BUD** → Approved Budget

### Purpose
Compares the current forecast or plan against:
- Historical performance (PY)
- Original budget (BUD)


---

## 4️⃣ Accuracy

### Purpose
Measures **forecast quality**.

### What It Shows
- Difference between forecasted values and actual results
- Accuracy percentages
- Variance analysis


---

## 5️⃣ Reporting Views – Corporate vs Business Unit vs Function

These views allow the same report to be analyzed at **different organizational levels**.


###  Corporate View

#### Scope
- Entire company
- Consolidated financial data



###  Business Unit (BU) View

#### Scope
- Individual business units or divisions



###  Function View

#### Scope
- Functional departments (HR, IT, Finance, Sales, etc.)



---



This structure ensures **clear, consistent, and multi-level financial reporting** in SAP Analytics Cloud. 



# Planning Section – Financial Forecast (SAP Analytics Cloud)

The **Planning** section is used for **data entry, forecasting, and financial modeling**. 
It allows FP&A teams to build a **complete financial forecast** across P&L, Balance Sheet–related items, and Cash Flow.

> Note: Supportive planning reports such as **CCOP vs BPOP** and **Sanity Checks** are excluded.



## 1️⃣ Gross Margin

### Purpose
Plans **revenue, cost of sales, and margin**.

### Why It Is Used
- Core profitability planning
- Assess margin impact of volume and pricing changes

---

## 2️⃣ Fixed Costs & FTEs

### Purpose
Plans **operating expenses and headcount**.

### Why It Is Used
- Workforce and cost control
- Understand cost structure evolution

---

## 3️⃣ Capitalizations

### Purpose
Plans which costs are **capitalized instead of expensed**.

### What Is Planned
- Capitalized project costs
- Asset creation costs

### Why It Is Used
- Impacts EBITDA positively
- Aligns accounting treatment with investment strategy

---

## 4️⃣ CapEx (Capital Expenditure)

### Purpose
Plans **investments in long-term assets**.

### Why It Is Used
- Control investment spending
- Support long-term growth planning

---

## 5️⃣ D&A (Depreciation & Amortization)

### Purpose
Plans depreciation and amortization of **existing assets**.

### Why It Is Used
- Reflects asset consumption
- Impacts EBIT calculations

---

## 6️⃣ New CapEx D&A

### Purpose
Calculates depreciation for **newly planned CapEx**.

### Why It Is Used
- Automatically derives future expenses
- Ensures accurate long-term forecasting

---

## 7️⃣ New Initiatives

### Purpose
Plans **strategic and transformation initiatives**.


### Why It Is Used
- Evaluate business cases
- Assess financial impact of strategic decisions

---

## 8️⃣ Below EBIT

### Purpose
Plans financial items **below EBIT**.

### What Is Planned
- Interest income and expense
- Taxes
- One-off or exceptional items

---

## 9️⃣ Cash Flow

### Purpose
Plans **cash inflows and outflows**.

### What Is Planned
- Operating cash flow
- Investing cash flow
- Financing cash flow
