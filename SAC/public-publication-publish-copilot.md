# ✅ Public Version vs Private Version vs Publishing vs Publication in SAC

## 1️⃣ Public Version
A **Public Version** is a version of data that everyone with access to the **model or story** can see.

### Typically used for:
- Finalized planning data (e.g., **Actuals, Budget, Forecast**)
- Shared numbers between teams

### When you update a public version (if you have write access):
- The changes are visible to others **immediately**

### Use case:
> You want your updated **Q2 forecast numbers** to be visible to your entire planning team.

---

## 2️⃣ Private Version
A **Private Version** is only visible to **you**.

### It’s used for:
- Trying out scenarios
- Making draft changes
- Testing predictions or adjustments before sharing

You can convert a private version into a public version, **but only if you have permissions**.

### Use case:
> You want to explore **“What if sales grow by 20%?”** without affecting shared data.

---

## 3️⃣ Publishing (Publishing a Version)
**Publishing** is the act of taking your **private version** and committing it to a **public version**.

### After publishing:
- Your changes **override** the public version
- Others will see your updates
- You need the **“Publish” authorization**

### Use case:
> After finishing your draft budget in a private version, you publish it to finalize it for the team.

---

## 4️⃣ Publication (Story Publication)
**Publication** in SAC refers to creating a **scheduled or static copy of a Story** and sending it to people.

### Common formats:
- PDF
- PowerPoint
- Snapshot view

🔹 This is about **sharing the report**, not the data version.  
🔹 Can be **automated using scheduling**.

### Use case:
> You schedule a weekly **PDF of the Sales Performance Story** to be emailed to managers.
